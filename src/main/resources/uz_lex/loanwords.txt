# These are stem-locked, not fully protected: "telefonlar" must still reduce to
# "telefon", but "zaryadka" must NOT reduce to "zaryad" — its final -ka is part
# of the Russian stem, while -ka is also the Uzbek dative allomorph after a
# voiceless stop. Layer 4 may strip affixes AFTER these stems; it may not cut
# into them.
#
# The -ka / -kа family is the dangerous one and is listed most fully. Words with
# harmless endings are included only where they are frequent enough in catalogue
# text to be worth an early exit.
#
