Linguistic resources
--------------------
src/main/resources/uz_lex/function_words.txt is derived from the
non-affixed stem list of UzMorphAnalyser by Ulugbek Salaev, used under the
MIT License. https://github.com/UlugbekSalaev/UzMorphAnalyser

src/main/resources/uz_lex/brand-exemptions.txt is generated against the
UzbekLemmaStems-POS-Dataset by Maksud Sharipov et al., used under the
Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/MaksudSharipov/UzbekLemmaStems-POS-Dataset

Orthographic conventions for the 2026 Latin alphabet, and the U+015F versus
U+0219 distinction, follow the analysis published in the alifbo project
(MIT License). No code from that project is used here.
https://github.com/azakapro/alifbo

brands.txt and loanwords.txt are seed lists compiled for this project.
